The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It orbits at an average
distance of 384,400 km and completes one orbit every 27.3 days.

The Moon's surface is covered in craters, mountains, and plains.
Its lack of atmosphere means temperature swings from -173°C at night
to 127°C during the day.

The Moon was likely formed 4.5 billion years ago from debris left
over after a Mars-sized body collided with early Earth. This is
known as the Giant Impact Hypothesis.
